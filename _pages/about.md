Hi, and thank you for visiting my personal webpage!

I'm a machine learning researcher and software engineer with a PhD from Aston University, based in Luxembourg.
I currently lead the Metrology Department at an industrial 3D-scanner manufacturer, where I build the verification
and certification tooling behind production scanners. Alongside that I work as an independent AI/ML consultant,
building LLM agents, retrieval systems and voice interfaces.

Before that I worked in research for about eight years, first in Novosibirsk and then at the Aston Institute of Photonic
Technologies, working on nonlinear signal processing and machine learning for fibre-optic communications. That work
is described on the [publications](/publications/) and [talks](/talks/) pages.

If you are looking for technical guidance, a collaborator, or someone to build an AI or measurement system,
you are welcome to reach out through the links in the sidebar.

What I work on
======

**Metrology and 3D scanning**  
I lead verification and certification of production 3D scanners against standards such as VDI/VDE 2634-3 and
ISO/IEC 17025. That covers the C++/Python software stack for automated processing and report generation, repeatability
and reproducibility studies, root-cause investigations of accuracy regressions, and point-cloud and geometric
analysis tools.

**AI agents and LLM systems**  
Multi-agent systems (planner / executor / critic), retrieval-augmented generation, and AI-augmented development
workflows for code review, specifications and testing, built with tools such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, n8n and FastAPI.
I have also built LLM assistants for messaging platforms and enterprise knowledge bases.

**Voice and real-time interaction**  
End-to-end conversational pipelines combining speech recognition, synthesis and environment-adaptive voice activity
detection. I designed the AI and voice stack for real-time NPCs in immersive VR, running at under 500 ms latency.

**Computer vision and audio ML**  
Segmentation, detection, classification and feature extraction for applied problems, including photometric
camera and lens models.

**Optical communications research**  
My PhD work covered the Nonlinear Fourier Transform (NFT) for signal transmission and restoration in long-haul fibre
systems, neural networks as fast alternatives to numerical NFT, and gradient-boosting and forest-based equalisers for
coherent transmission. I also wrote [hpcom](https://github.com/esf0/hpcom), a GPU-accelerated fibre-link simulator.
