The random process corresponding to the information content of a conventional optical WDM signal statistically creates conditions leading to soliton occurrence.
We quantify the statistics of soliton component occurrence in standard telecom signals, considering a return-to-zero pulse carrying multiple WDM channels with QPSK, 16-, 64- and 1024-QAM modulation.
See also the related [OFDM](/publication/2018-12-15-soliton-content) and [OFDM/WDM return-to-zero](/publication/2020-01-15-nonlinear-spectrum-ofdm-wdm) studies.
